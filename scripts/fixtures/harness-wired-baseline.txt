test-target-clause.sh          [local]     AF-346's own harness. Every cell compiles for real, so wiring it into the required `checks` gate adds minutes to every lane's push. Its cheap half is scripts/test-selector-clauses.sh, which IS wired.
test-lineage-render.sh         [local]     AF-677: needs a LIVE server carrying real, aged board history to prove its renderer against `/api/why/task/<id>`'s ACTUAL payload rather than a synthetic fixture -- the script's own header calls this deliberate ("a synthetic fixture would not prove the renderer matches what the endpoint actually emits"). It self-reports SKIP (exit 2) when unreachable, and a GitHub-hosted runner has nothing at localhost:8824 by construction, so wiring it would either always skip or need CI to stand up production-shaped historical data neither this repo nor any runner has. Stays a local dev-box tool; re-run it by hand against a real box before trusting a Lineage-tab change.
